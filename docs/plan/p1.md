## Feature: T1.5 /api/meeting/summarize

**Goal:** Implement `POST /api/meeting/summarize` analyzing Romanian meeting transcripts into plain-language summaries, explicit decisions with source quotes, action items assigned to people (`who`) with deadlines, and unclear points, strictly without emotion/sentiment inference or audio processing.
**Non-goals:** Audio capture or speech recognition (browser Web Speech API owned by P2 in `speech.js` / T2.5); inferring speaker emotions or interpersonal sentiment (banned under EU AI Act).
