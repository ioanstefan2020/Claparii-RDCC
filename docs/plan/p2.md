## Feature: Needs Profile & AI Transparency Notice Screen (T2.2)

### 1. Goal & Non-goals
- **Goal**: Accessible employee settings screen (`web/employee/profile.html`) and client module (`web/shared/profile.js`) enabling users to customize AI simplification preferences (`reading_level`, `format`, `max_sentence_words`, `read_aloud_default`, and own-words needs notes up to 500 characters) and visual display preferences (`theme`, `textSize`, `spacing`, `simpleMode`), backed by an explicit plain-language Romanian transparency notice and an irreversible local data wipe button.
- **Non-goals**:
  - NO medical diagnosis, clinical condition, or disability category fields (users describe functional workplace needs in their own words only).
  - NO server-side persistence, accounts, or user tracking; all data remains in client-side `localStorage`.
  - NO emotion inference from voice/face or tone classification on employee profile.
  - NO external CDN scripts or stylesheet dependencies; pure vanilla HTML/CSS/ES modules.

### 2. Interfaces & Contracts
- `PROFILE_KEY = "primazi_user_profile"`, `A11Y_KEY = "primazi_a11y_prefs"`
- `DEFAULT_PROFILE`:
  - `reading_level`: `"standard" | "simple" | "very_simple"` (default: `"simple"`)
  - `format`: `"steps" | "short_paragraphs"` (default: `"steps"`)
  - `needs_notes`: string <= 500 chars (own words, non-medical)
  - `max_sentence_words`: integer between 6 and 30 (default: 12)
  - `read_aloud_default`: boolean (default: false)
- Functions: `getProfile()`, `saveProfile(updates)`, `getApiProfile(profile)`, `getA11yPrefs()`, `saveA11yPrefs(prefs)`, `applyA11yPrefs(prefs)`, `deleteAllUserData()`.
