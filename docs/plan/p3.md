## Feature: Employer Document Analysis (T3.4 — F6)

### Goal
Analyze employer documents (onboarding guides, job ads, internal policies) for clarity, cognitive accessibility, and inclusive practices:
1. Deterministic readability statistics computed in pure Python (sentence count, avg words/sentence, long sentences, 0-100 score). Scores the **document**, never a person.
2. Clarity issues with verbatim quotes verified via `app.guardrails.verify_quotes`.
3. Easy-read rewrite (`easy_read_ro`) in plain Romanian.
4. Recommended adjustment checklist mapped to catalog IDs.

### Non-goals
- Scoring or evaluating candidates/employees (strict responsible-AI boundary: score documents only).
- Diagnosing or inferring disability types.
- Free-form unsupported legal claims.

### Interfaces
CONTRACTS §3.6:
`POST /api/employer/analyze`
Request: `{"text": "...", "doc_type": "onboarding"}`
Response: `SuccessEnvelope[EmployerAnalyzeData]`
Readability: `sentence_count`, `avg_sentence_words`, `long_sentences`, `score_0_100`.
Issues: `kind` (`jargon | long_sentence | missing_deadline | vague_instruction | missing_structure`), `quote`, `fix`.
`easy_read_ro`: string.
`adjustment_checklist`: list of `{"id": "...", "title": "..."}`.
