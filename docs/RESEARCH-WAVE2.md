# RESEARCH-WAVE2 — what we checked online, what we decided, and the idea bank (frozen reference)

## 1. Findings that change what we build (sources checked 22 Oct 2026)
| Topic | What we found | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Audio → text** | Groq hosts Whisper (`whisper-large-v3-turbo`, `whisper-large-v3`) behind an OpenAI-compatible `/audio/transcriptions`; Romanian (`ro`) supported; formats incl. mp3, mp4, m4a, wav, webm, ogg, flac; free-tier upload limit 25 MB; recommended pre-processing is 16 kHz mono via ffmpeg; **SRT/VTT not returned**; per-segment timestamps via `verbose_json`; 429s possible (queue). (GroqDocs speech-to-text) | `/api/transcribe` on Groq Whisper with ffmpeg shrink + chunking; we generate SRT/VTT from segments; local faster-whisper as optional fallback |
| **Natural-sounding TTS** | Edge (Chromium) exposes Microsoft "Online (Natural)" neural voices through the normal `speechSynthesis` API (online); Azure has Romanian neural voices *Alina* and *Emil*. Chrome desktop ships only ~19 Google voices across 15 languages (Romanian not guaranteed). Piper (MIT, offline, CPU) has a Romanian voice `ro_RO-mihai-medium` (CC0 data, 1 male speaker, medium quality). | TTS engine order: (1) browser natural Romanian voice if present (**demo in Edge**), (2) server Piper `mihai`, (3) any ro-RO browser voice. Quality levers that matter more than the engine: Romanian text normalisation, sentence-by-sentence speech, pauses, rate 0.9–0.95, follow-along highlight. Optional paid cloud voice only via env key, never required |
| **Bionic reading** | No benefit found: Snell 2024 (*Acta Psychologica*, "No, Bionic Reading does not work"), Readwise test with 2,074 readers (no effect on speed/comprehension), 2025 eye-tracking with 90 adults (no change in speed, skipping or comprehension); one small pilot with learners with specific learning disorders suggests possible decoding-accuracy gains but is not conclusive. Specialist dyslexia typefaces repeatedly fail to beat ordinary ones; individual preference differs. | Keep as an **optional personal preference**, off by default, **never claimed as beneficial**. Lead with things that are supported by guidelines: spacing, plain language, structure, reading support, user control |
| **Easy-to-read** | Inclusion Europe "Information for all" (European standards for easy-to-read, incl. written, electronic, video and audio information) + an Easy-to-read checklist. | `EASY_READ_RULES_RO` in `PROMPT-SPEC.md`; employer analyzer scores documents against the same rules; check the Romanian edition for wording |
| **Sarcasm / irony detection** | Hard for LLMs: needs context, tone/face cues are missing in text; one open-source comparison reports ~66–69% accuracy; an ACL 2026 paper reports models near-perfect on synthetic benchmarks but close to random guessing on organic human speech. | Implement **tentative pragmatic notes**, not "detection": conservative prompt, evidence quote required, only `low|medium` confidence, always "posibil … nesigur" for joke/irony/sarcasm, off by default, evaluated on our own labelled set; **text only, never from voice** (EU AI Act workplace emotion-inference ban). The more reliable and more useful notes are `indirect_request`, `figurative` (idioms) and `urgency` |
| **Highlighting** | General learning-science reviews rate highlighting as low-utility when overused; meaningful, sparse, consistent cues help. (From the literature on highlighting; not re-verified today.) | Density caps (5/10/15%), categories with legend, non-colour cues, one-click off |
| **Cognitive accessibility** | W3C COGA guidance and WCAG 2.2 emphasise predictable layout, plain language, clear steps, no time limits, error prevention, help available, minimal distraction. | Focus levels 0–3, guided/card layouts, no timeouts, inline confirmation, calm mode |

## 2. Honest limits to tell the jury
Tone notes are suggestions, not facts. Highlights and reading aids are preferences. Romanian TTS quality depends on the device/browser. We tested with the people and devices listed in `docs/evidence/` and nothing else. Legal/rights info is curated and informational.

Note: time to complete, where they got stuck, which settings they changed, quotes. Change one thing per session. Record in `docs/evidence/`.
